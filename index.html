MOUZ vs Vitality

BEFORE THE MATCH

Combat Power

Every team carries a rating earned from its results. Combat Power turns the gap between two of them into a win probability, before a single minion spawns.

  • Win probability for any two teams, drawing on more than 80,000 League of Legends games and more than 20,000 CS2 maps
  • Matchup reading and the inputs behind each win probability
  • Team Dex, with base stats, roster and win conditions

DURING THE MATCH

Kairos

A second screen for the match you are watching or casting. Kairos follows the game state live and marks the moments when the match could turn.

  • Live chart with Kairos markers, beside your broadcast
  • Event log and scoreboard: teamfights and objectives in League of Legends, rounds and economy in CS2
  • Moments marked on the chart as they happen, with an alert by email. SMS to follow.
  • Match reviews afterwards, for the wrong calls as well as the right ones
