What is Kairos in esports?

Kairos means the right or critical moment. In KOLMO's esports coverage, it is a way to examine when a change in a match creates a meaningful opportunity and whether the following play supports that interpretation.

By KOLMO · Updated September 22, 2026

From a moment to its consequences

The word comes from the Ancient Greek καιρός. For someone watching a match, it invites a practical question: what became possible at this moment, and what happened next?

A change becomes easier to understand when you connect the earlier situation, the action that changed it, and the opportunities a team then used.

Examples in League of Legends and CS2

These are illustrative scenarios, rather than claims about a recorded match.

  • League of Legends: a team removes one opponent before an objective fight. Look at how that numbers advantage affects positioning, access to the objective and the next play.
  • Counter-Strike 2: an opening elimination creates space on one side of the map. Look at whether the team converts that space into site access and how the opponents respond.

How to examine a possible turning point

  1. Establish the prior situation. What opportunities and constraints did each team face?
  2. Identify the change. What action or event created a different opportunity?
  3. Follow the consequences. Did the next plays support a lasting change, or was the opportunity short-lived?
  4. State the evidence and limits. Separate what the match shows from your interpretation of it.

Real-time detection and post-match review

A real-time detection claim needs a recorded output from that moment. A post-match review explains a sequence with later events available. Any analysis should make clear which of these it is.

Kairos is a concept for understanding a match. A probability or performance claim needs its own method and supporting evidence. The terminal on our homepage displays simulated preview values.
